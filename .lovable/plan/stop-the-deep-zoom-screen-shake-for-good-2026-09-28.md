# Stop the deep-zoom screen shake for good

## What's actually wrong

I traced the camera code, and the shake isn't a tuning problem — two different owners move the view every single frame, and they disagree:

1. **The follow lock forces its own zoom level back.** When a star is selected, every frame the camera is re-centred on it *at the zoom level captured when it was selected*. So while you wheel-zoom, your new zoom is overwritten a fraction of a second later, then applied again, then overwritten. That alternation is the shake, and it never settles because neither side gives up.
2. **The camera move lands one frame later than the star move.** Stars are drawn from the animation clock, but the camera is handed to the zoom/pan library, which applies it on its next update. So the camera is always chasing where the star *was*.

Both effects are multiplied by zoom. Deep stars ride on their whole parent chain, so a tiny position difference gets magnified by the deep zoom factor — a barely visible wobble at the top generation becomes a violent shake at the seventh, exactly what you're seeing.

## The fix

- **One owner for the view.** The follow camera keeps the selected star centred but always respects your current zoom level, never restoring an older one. Wheel and pinch change zoom; the follow logic only ever handles centring.
- **Same-frame camera.** The view is positioned directly in the same frame the stars are drawn, instead of being queued through the zoom library, so the camera can no longer trail the star it is following. The library is re-synced only when a gesture ends, so pinch, wheel, panning and the zoom buttons keep working normally.
- **Deep-zoom steadiness.** Centring is computed in the star's own local family space (position relative to its parent chain) so magnified rounding differences can't accumulate, and position updates below a fraction of a pixel on screen are ignored instead of ping-ponging.
- **Gesture handover stays as it is.** Zooming keeps the selected star locked; only deliberate panning releases it. Chat Mode keeps its own framing rules.
- No star is created, removed, resized or re-generated by any of this — it stays presentation-only.

## Technical notes

- `GeneratorSystem.tsx`: the follow `useLayoutEffect` stops passing `f.scale` and instead reads the live scale (`viewScaleRef` / `stateRef`), using the captured scale only for the initial 650ms glide; once the glide finishes, scale is entirely user-owned.
- Replace per-frame `setTransform` with a direct transform write on the `TransformComponent` content element (ref), and push the final values back into the library state on `onWheelStop` / `onPanningStop` / `onPinchStop` / glide end so its internal bookkeeping stays correct.
- Add a dead-band: skip the write when the centring delta is under ~0.25 screen px.
- Compute the follow target from the parent-chain-relative position at the current scale to avoid large-magnitude float cancellation at scale 96.
- Mirror the same change in `SolarSystem.tsx`, which shares this camera code.
